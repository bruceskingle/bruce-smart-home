# Bruce's Smart Home

<img src="images/app-icon.png" alt="Bruce's Smart Home" width="128" align="right">

**Bruce's Smart Home, the Energy Explorer**, is a desktop app for macOS and Windows which shows you the data your energy supplier holds about you: your bills and what each part of them cost, your smart meter's readings, and, where your supplier has them, more detailed readings, such as an in-home device's every few seconds, and your car's charging sessions. It keeps a copy of everything it downloads, so it is quick to look at again, and still there after the supplier stops providing it.

Each supplier is supported by a module, and it supports two:

- **[Octopus Energy](help/octopus/index.md)**, in the UK: your bills, your meters' half hourly readings, the Home Mini's readings every 10 seconds, and your car's smart charging sessions.
- **[GloBird Energy](help/globird/index.md)**, in Australia: your bills, your smart meter's readings every 5 minutes, and each day's costs.

Modules for devices in your home, a [Tesla](help/tesla/index.md) Powerwall gateway and a [myenergi](help/myenergi/index.md) zappi, add what they measured, so that the app can show what your house, your car and your batteries each used.
