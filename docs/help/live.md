# Live

Home's Live page draws the site's [topology](site.md), as the Site page does, with what each circuit is taking or giving now: the grid importing or exporting, the Powerwalls charging or discharging, the solar panels making, and each load, e.g. the car and the house. Dots move along the lines between them in the direction the energy flows, from what feeds a circuit into it, or back out of it, e.g. from the solar panels, faster the more power there is. Each figure is in watts, as it is, so that they add up: what comes into a board, e.g. the gateway, is what goes out of it, to within a watt or so of rounding. Dots only move for 10 W or more, so that a few watts either way, e.g. the gateway's site meter, which the Powerwalls hold at about zero, does not look like a flow. Solar panels making less than 50 W are taken as making none, as their meter reads a few watts at night, and the house's figure takes the difference, so they still add up.

Each circuit's power is the average over the latest minute the devices have measured: from its own channel, or for the circuit of a board without one, the board's less its other circuits'. The grid, if nothing the app reads measures it directly, e.g. only the Octopus meter, whose readings come later, is the total of the circuits it feeds, and **not known** if one of them is.

While the page is shown, the app reads the Tesla readings from the [capture devices](devices.md) every 10 seconds, a few seconds after each of their polls, rather than every 5 minutes, so it is no more than about 15 seconds behind; **As of** says when the power is of. The zappi's readings come from myenergi as whole minutes every few minutes, so the car's power, and anything worked out from it, e.g. the grid's, is its latest minute's, with when that was under it, e.g. **as of 16:41:00**. A car charging changes little over a few minutes. Each read is in [the log](log.md), so while the page is shown the log has a few lines every 10 seconds.

Until the site's topology is set, on the [Site](site.md) page, there is nothing to draw.
