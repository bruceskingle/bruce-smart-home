# GloBird

The GloBird module fetches a GloBird Energy customer's electricity data from GloBird's customer portal. It is new:

- **Meters**, the meters GloBird lists for the account, and the registers of the smart meter's readings, **E1** what was imported from the grid and **B1** what was exported to it. Under them, the latest **Rates**, as the Rates page shows them. Click a register to see its readings every 5 minutes, by the tariff's charge types, e.g. Peak Usage and Offpeak Usage, as a graph or a table, as on Octopus's page of [a meter's readings](../octopus/meter.md). The graph's **Cost** shows what they cost, in AUD, worked out by the app, as GloBird gives only each day's costs: each reading's kWh at its charge type's rate, which the Rates page works out from those costs, and E1's share of the day's supply charge, as GloBird charged it, every 5 minutes. A reading whose charge type has no rate, e.g. one too little used to tell, has no cost. B1's are the feed-in credits, below zero. A day's costs can differ by a cent or so from GloBird's, and more where GloBird charged some of a day's kWh on another day, or in summer, when the days here start an hour before GloBird's. Home's [Electricity](../electricity.md) page puts the import and export together. Times and days are Sydney's, by the clock. It starts at **5min**, each reading as GloBird gave it; at a longer period the readings are added up, so the table's kWh are in <span class="derived">magenta</span>, as numbers the app works out are. **Reload** fetches the latest readings from GloBird.
- **Rates**, your tariff's rates in cents a kWh, and its daily charge, as GloBird charges them, newest first, in <span class="derived">magenta</span>. GloBird's portal does not give them, so the app works them out from each day's costs and the meter's kWh. Each day's cost is rounded to the cent, so the rate of a charge type you use little, e.g. Peak Usage if batteries run the house then, is less exact, and one with too few kWh is not known. When the rates change, e.g. at GloBird's review each 1 July, a new set starts from that day.
- **Days**, each day's energy from the grid by the tariff's charge types, e.g. Peak Usage and Offpeak Usage, the energy exported, and the day's costs by category, in AUD. The readings and costs are fetched from GloBird when the Meters, Rates or Days page opens, if they were last fetched a day or more ago, the days not cached yet and the last few days again, as the meter's readings arrive a day or so late; the page says when they were fetched. GloBird keeps only about 40 days of readings, so if they have not been fetched for 20 days, Home's [Electricity](../electricity.md) page says so, with when those not fetched start to be lost, and a link to the GloBird pages.
  - Numbers GloBird gave are in the usual colour; those the app works out, the number of readings and the total, are in <span class="derived">magenta</span>.
  - Hold the pointer over a day's **USAGE** or **SOLAR** cost to see its split by charge type at the day's rates, from the Rates page.
  - **Check** says when a day's charges do not fit the rates, and why if it can, e.g. that GloBird charged for fewer kWh than the meter measured that day, as it sometimes moves kWh from one day to the next. Such days are left out when the rates are worked out.
- **Bills**, the bills GloBird has emailed, newest first: when each was emailed, and its invoice, period and total, read from its PDF, which are in <span class="scraped">yellow</span>, as values read from a document are, rather than given by GloBird's API. The list of emails is fetched from GloBird when the page opens, if it is a day old, to find new bills, a request or two. Click a bill's invoice to see it in a window of its own, beside the list, or **Fetch**, for a bill whose PDF is not cached yet, which fetches it, with its email first, two requests, then opens it. Each bill is fetched once, as it never changes. A bill's window shows:
  - how many of its days are cached, as the days' figures are only all of the bill's when every day is;
  - its charges as the bill says them, e.g. Peak Usage 5.83 kWh at 37.18c, in <span class="scraped">yellow</span>, beside the meter's kWh of that charge type over the bill's days, and the number of days, for the daily charge, in <span class="derived">magenta</span>, as the app works them out;
  - the bill's charges by the categories of GloBird's daily costs, e.g. USAGE, beside what the days' costs of each add up to, and the difference;
  - **View PDF**, the bill itself, in another window of its own. The windows are the app's own, listed in the Window menu, and Escape closes them. On Linux the PDF opens in the system's PDF viewer instead.

  The days' figures are only all of the bill's when every one of its days is cached, which the window says. GloBird keeps only a few weeks of readings, so bills from before the module first fetched have no days to compare.
- **Cache Files**, every file it has fetched, as GloBird sent it.

Days run from midnight to midnight in Australian Eastern Standard Time (AEST, UTC+10) all year, without daylight saving, as GloBird's days and tariff times do, following the electricity market. So in summer, when the clocks in New South Wales, Victoria, the ACT and Tasmania go forward, a day starts at 1am by the clock there, and a peak of 3pm to 9pm is 4pm to 10pm by the clock.

GloBird keeps only a few weeks of readings, so the app keeps every day it fetches. Open the Meters or Days page at least every few weeks, or the days in between are lost.

[Logging in](login.md) explains what it needs to fetch your data.
